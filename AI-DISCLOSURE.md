# AI Usage Disclosure

Honest, consistent wording for how generative AI is used in these mods — for the
[ficsit.app / SMR](https://ficsit.app/content-policy) upload form (which has a
mandatory AI-disclosure field) and for the top of a mod's README.

---

## Primary blurb — README top / SMR field

> This mod uses Claude (Anthropic) as a coding assistant. All AI-suggested code is
> reviewed and tested by a human before it's included. No art is AI-generated — all
> models, textures, audio, and icons are human-made.

---

## Longer version — if a fuller note is wanted

> Claude (Anthropic) is used as a coding assistant during development: scaffolding,
> refactoring, and explaining the SML/Unreal APIs. Every AI suggestion is read,
> edited as needed, and compiled and tested in-game by a human before being
> committed; the author takes full responsibility for the result.
>
> No art is AI-generated. All art assets — models, textures, audio, and icons — are
> human-made. AI is used here only for code, never for artwork.

---

## Why this exists

- SMR requires disclosing generative-AI usage on upload — a disclosure, not a ban.
- Anthropic assigns output ownership to the user, so there's no obstacle to sharing.
